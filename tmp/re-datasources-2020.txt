Data sources for mainstream QR funding
Our mainstream quality-related research (QR) funding is allocated selectively according to quality.

The source data we have used to calculate mainstream QR funding for 2020-21 has not changed since the 2015-16 mainstream QR funding
allocations. We use data from the 2014 Research Excellence Framework (REF 2014), with the volume measure calculated from Category A FTE
staff submitted to REF 2014 attributable to quality levels 3* and 4*.

In the mainstream QR funding Excel file the column headed 'Quality-weighted volume' contains the volume measure multiplied by the quality
weighting. For 2020-21, the following quality weightings were applied to the REF 2014 quality sub-profiles:

 Quality level           4*                       3*                         2*                       1*               Unclassified

 Quality weighting       4                        1                          0                        0                0
